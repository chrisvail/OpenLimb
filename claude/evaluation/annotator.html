Backspace / Delete: clear active point. S: save. N / P: next / previous mesh.
Circumference: 3 points on the circumference plane (any 3 around the limb). Width: 2 points at the two extremes (medial / lateral) at the right height. Length: 2 points. See the reference limb (bottom right) for where the SSM puts them.
